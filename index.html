Charles Nessler

Karl Ludwig Nessler · 1872–1951

A pioneer of the permanent wave, whose life and work connect European hairdressing, technological invention and the growth of the modern beauty industry.

This page brings together three independent, complementary research websites. Each covers a different aspect of Nessler's history, with its own documents, sources and historical focus.

Three websites, one historical story

Nessler's career extended across countries and decades. Rather than repeat the same biography in several places, these websites divide the research into connected areas.

Why three separate websites?

The division reflects the scope of the surviving historical material: personal biography and early inventions, international career and writings, and later industrial history. Together, the three sites offer a more detailed picture than a single introductory page could provide. This portal is a guide to that research, not a fourth biography or a duplicate archive.

Independent archival research

Part of an international research initiative

The International Hairdressing History Project documents the history of hairdressing, its inventors, techniques, tools and professional networks. It places Nessler's work alongside other figures and developments in the history of permanent waving.

For primary sources, detailed references and further reading, please consult the individual research websites linked above.
